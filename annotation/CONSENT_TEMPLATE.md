# Annotation Consent and Acknowledgment Template

Project: IEEE Access calibration audit (Brick holdout)

This form is a template for human review and must be completed, approved, and retained according to the responsible institution's requirements before annotation begins. It is not evidence that consent has been obtained.

## Information provided to the annotator
- Purpose: evaluate whether supplied material contains a verbatim evidence span supporting each competency-question answer.
- Task mode: some annotators (per the registered protocol version they sign) review machine-generated draft labels and confirm or revise them (AI-assisted annotation, registered 2026-10-07, PREREG addendum 11); draft origin and timestamps are permanently recorded and disclosed in the publication. Other annotators (e.g. the registered blind subsample) receive no drafts and label independently — the mode for each role is fixed before participation.
- Workload: a planned primary pass and, if applicable, a separate washout re-score; exact schedule is agreed before participation.
- Limits: the annotator is not an independent expert reference standard; variant identity may be inferable; results are a calibration audit.
- Data handling: completed sheets and notes are stored securely, de-identified for release, and access is restricted to the study team.
- Voluntary participation: the annotator may decline or stop without penalty, subject to the applicable institutional process.
- Contact and review information: to be completed by the human project owner.
