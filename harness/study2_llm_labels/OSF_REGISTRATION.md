**Title.** Can LLM evidence labels on a full-rendering view stand in for external human raters in an audit of an LLM answerability judge? A two-stage, gated study on an ontology-extraction benchmark.

## Study information

**Description.** An earlier study of ontology-extraction variants (A-F) scores each (variant, competency question) cell with an LLM judge (answerability 0-3) and audits the judge with human evidence labels S (does a verbatim span of the rendering support an answer?). The audit statistic is the silent over-credit rate pi = P(J >= 2 and S = 0). Human raters are scarce, so we test whether LLM raters can supply S. A previous attempt, with subgraph views, agreed with the two human raters at kappa <= 0.16; the views held the expected-answer terms in only 0-5% of the disagreement cells, a likely confound. Study 2 gives the LLM rater a retrieval view drawn from the full rendering and runs a pre-fixed calibration against the humans (Stage 1). Only if it passes does it run an expanded audit on a new question bank (Stage 2).

**Hypotheses (directional, falsifiable).**
- H1 (Stage 1): for at least one primary LLM labeler, the lower 95% bound of kappa(LLM label, human) is at least kappa(human, human) - 0.10 on the same 188 cells, for each of the two human raters.
- H2 (Stage 2, conditional on H1): in the expanded audit the eight acceptance gates of the parent pre-registration (G1-G8, same thresholds, with the stated analogues) are computed on LLM labels; no direction is hypothesised for individual gates. Descriptive otherwise.
H1 failing is a reportable result and ends the labeled audit.

## Design plan

**Study type.** Observational / measurement-validation study on existing data plus new model calls. No human participants. **Blinding.** The LLM raters are blind to judge scores and rationales, variant names, human labels and the expected answers; the judge's quoted span is located in the rendering and its neighborhood is part of the retrieval view, without marking. **Study design.** Stage 1: 188 cells (33 question clusters) x 3 labeler models x 25 sampled labels. Stage 2: all eligible cells of an expanded bank (target >= 60 eligible question clusters) x primary labelers that passed x 25 labels; two non-Qwen judges score the cells.
**Randomization.** Cell processing order is a seeded shuffle (seed 20260913); the bank is not subsampled.

## Sampling plan

**Existing data.** The 188 primary holdout cells of the parent study, with labels from two human raters and the registered judge's scores (already collected; the study team had seen aggregate agreement of an earlier LLM-label attempt but no per-cell comparison for this study). The expanded bank is an unreviewed AI-drafted set of 84 questions (6 categories x 14) written on 2026-10-13 from the Brick 1.4.4 snapshot only; it is frozen as is (sha256 recorded before any judge call), none deleted. **Data collection procedure.** Model requests to three open-weight models on shared servers (request only). **Sample size.** Stage 1: 188 cells. Stage 2: eligible cells = (variant, question) pairs whose grounding terms are present in the variant's rendering (the parent study's strict eligibility rule); target >= 60 eligible clusters; if fewer, the target is not lowered and the result is labeled "target not met". **Stopping rule.** Stage 2 is not run as an audit if the Stage 1 gate fails. Model runs stop on HTTP 402/403, a long 429, or five consecutive failed calls; token and wall-clock caps for the secondary model only.

## Variables

**Manipulated.** None (variant is a design factor of the parent study). **Measured.** Per slot: S in {0,1} (supported by a verbatim span in the view the model saw), R in 0-3 (answerability of the shown material; R = 0 is allowed, no floor), the cited span, call latency. Per cell: S = majority of the valid S slots of 25 (needs >= 15 valid; a tie leaves the cell undefined); R = lower median. Judge score J in 0-3 (median of three repeats) from the audited judge. **Indices.** pi-hat (silent over-credit), coverage on L1* = J if S = 1 else min(J, 1), A->B contrast on L1*, kappa, QWK, MAE.
**Labeler view.** Deterministic retrieval from the full rendering: passages around every line matching the question's grounding terms (the parent study's namespace-agnostic matcher), the judge's quoted span located in the rendering, +-600 characters of context rounded to whole lines, lines cut at 4,000 characters, total at most 100,000 characters (about 48k tokens at most); a question-term fallback when fewer than 2,000 characters are found. The share of views that contain the expected-answer terms is reported (target >= 90%, reported whatever it is).
**Labelers.** GLM-4.7-Flash and gpt-oss-20b (primary), Qwen3.8-Flash-Next (secondary, same family as the extractor; never in a gate). Five role personas x five seeds, temperature 0.7. The labeling instruction is the parent study's criteria text with two insertions (material description; R may be 0) -- exact wording in `PLAN.md` 32.5.

## Analysis plan

**Statistical models.** Question-clustered BCa bootstrap, 95%, B = 10,000, seed 20260913, for every interval (the parent study's analyzer).
**Stage 1 gate (fixed).** For each primary model m and human h in {AH, R2}: pass iff n >= 30 and the lower 95% bound of kappa(L_m, S_h) >= kappa(AH, R2) - 0.10, kappa(AH, R2) taken on the same cells. Stage 1 passes iff at least one primary model passes both humans. Also reported: pi-hat under LLM labels versus both humans (paper values: 7.4% and 13.8%), exact agreement, consistency-rule coverage (at least 23 of 25 agree), test-retest alpha, latency distribution, view coverage, and a negative control on the 24 registered ablated controls (diagnostic).
**Stage 2.** Bank provenance audit (written honestly before the freeze: when written, whether any artifact was seen, whether the grounding terms exist in the frozen snapshot), freeze by hash and tag, eligibility by the parent rule. Judges: the registered holdout judge has no route here, so the two non-Qwen judges (GLM-4.7-Flash, gpt-oss-20b) score the question-only subgraph views (a named deviation: the full rendering does not fit their window). Gates G1-G8 with the parent thresholds; analogues: G2 = split-half test-retest QWK of the LLM rating (>= 0.70 lower bound); G3 = >= 22 of 24 ablated controls labeled unsupported; G4 = fail if QWK(J, R) upper bound < 0.60; G5 = MAE(J, R) upper bound <= 0.50 under the R = 0 rubric; G6 = A->B consistency + other-judge direction + fragility K >= 6; G7 = every call logged with timestamp and latency, append-only raw files, and fewer than 3% of calls under 0.5 s (replacing the human dwell-time clause); G8 not run. Report: pi-hat with BCa, two-way error counts (over- and under-credit), MAE, the A->B contrast with its minimum detectable effect (2.8 x SD of paired differences / sqrt(pairs)), and a verbatim-span check of the judges' evidence.
**Inference criteria.** Stage 1 as above; Stage 2 gates with the parent thresholds, no multiplicity correction beyond the parent's.
**Data exclusion.** A cell whose prompt exceeds a model window is logged and excluded for that model; undefined labels are excluded and counted; nothing else.
**Missing data.** Invalid replies are retried twice, then the slot has no label; no imputation.
**Exploratory analysis.** Per-persona and per-variant breakdowns, the comparison with the subgraph-view labels of the earlier attempt, and the secondary model are descriptive.
