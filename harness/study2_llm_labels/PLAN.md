## 32.1 Status, question, claim ceiling

**Status.** A new, separately registered study. It reuses the paper's instruments (holdout renderings A-F, the registered rubric, the analyzer, the personas and criteria of addendum 30). It is **not** a confirmatory test of the paper's hypothesis and it replaces no human label of the paper. The earlier result (addendum 30, subgraph views): persona-ensemble LLM labels agreed with the two human raters at kappa <= 0.16, and the subgraph views held the bank's expected-answer terms in only 0-5% of the disagreement cells, a likely **view confound**. This study removes that confound by showing the LLM rater evidence drawn from the **full rendering**, and asks whether the calibrated LLM labels can then carry an expanded audit that no human rater will perform.

**The author's "next plan" has four parts; where each is fixed here.**
1. LLM raters blind to the judge output, with logged timing: 32.5 (blindness), 32.5 (latency per call), 32.9 (G7).
2. A question bank with >= 60 eligible clusters: 32.7.
3. A labeling rubric that allows R = 0: 32.5.
4. A pre-registration: this file and `OSF_REGISTRATION.md` (text for the author to paste into OSF; the study cannot be registered on OSF by the agents).

**Two stages, gated.** Stage 1 calibrates the LLM labeler against the two human raters on the 188 holdout cells. Only if the pre-fixed gate of 32.6 passes does Stage 2 (expanded audit on a new bank) run as an LLM-labeled audit. If Stage 1 fails, Stage 2 is not run as an audit: the report stops there, and judge scoring of the new bank may still be run descriptively (32.8) and is then reported as judge scores only, with no label-based statistic.

**Claim ceiling.** A passed gate says the labeler agrees with the humans nearly as well as the humans agree with each other **on these 188 cells and this view construction**. It does not say that the labels are accurate, that they transfer to another bank, or that they replace human labels. Every Stage 2 number is "LLM-labeled; not human-validated beyond Stage 1". A failed gate is reported as the result.

## 32.4 The labeler view (deterministic retrieval from the full rendering)

The LLM rater never sees the subgraph. Its material is a **retrieval view** `V(R, q, spans)` built from the full rendering `R` of the cell's variant by the rule below. It is a pure function of the rendering text, the bank row (`question`, `grounding`) and the located judge span(s); no model, no randomness.

**Parameters (fixed here):** `N = 600` characters of context; `LINE_CAP = 4000` characters; `CAP_CHARS = 100,000` characters (the view budget); `MIN_CHARS = 2,000`; `FALLBACK_CAP = 20,000`. The cap is set so that the prompt (view + about 4k tokens of system prompt) stays under 48k tokens for the 65,536-token window of the primary models; the token count is verified by the tokenizer preflight (rule F).

**Rule.**
* **A. Terms.** The anchors of the question are those of the paper's eligibility rule: `run_holdout._grounding_anchors(grounding)` plus the compound anchor when the grounding contains `>` or `~`, exactly as in `_grounding_match_info`. A rendering line **matches** if `_matching_line_numbers_namespace_agnostic` lists it (namespace- and case-agnostic). Lines are `R.splitlines(keepends=True)`.
* **B. Judge span.** The quoted evidence of the judge (Stage 1: the registered judge's `evidence` string of the cell; Stage 2: the evidence of **each** audited judge, union) is whitespace-normalised and searched in the whitespace-normalised `R`; if the whole string is not found, it is split at `...` and the ellipsis character and every piece of at least 20 normalised characters is searched; the first occurrence of each found piece is mapped back to the rendering lines it covers. These are the **span lines**. The score and the rationale of the judge are never used or shown. The span lines are not marked in the view and are ordered by document position like every other line.
* **C. Seeds and priority.** Seeds, in priority order: (P0) all span lines in document order; (P1) matching lines ranked by (number of distinct anchors present in the line, descending; line number, ascending). For each seed in that order, the seed line and then the lines at distance 1 before, 1 after, 2 before, 2 after, ... within the window of `N` characters before and after the seed line (rounded outward to whole lines) are added while the running total of view characters stays within `CAP_CHARS`; the first line that does not fit ends that seed. A line longer than `LINE_CAP` is cut to a contiguous window of `LINE_CAP` characters centred on the first anchor occurrence (or on the span start for a span line; start of line if there is none), so every shown string stays a verbatim substring of `R`.
* **D. Fallback.** If the total is below `MIN_CHARS` after C (for example an ablated control, whose anchor lines are removed), seeds are added from the lines that contain at least one content term of the **question text** (`grounding_check.content_terms(question, set())`), ranked by (number of distinct question terms, descending; line number, ascending), with the same window and line rules, until the total reaches `FALLBACK_CAP` or the candidates are exhausted. Nothing from the expected answer is used by the builder.
* **E. Output.** The included lines in document order; consecutive lines are joined as they are; between two groups that are not adjacent in `R` one line `[…]` is inserted. The text is placed in the user message exactly as in addendum 28 (`rater_luna.user_message(view, cell)`: the view, the Korean category, the question, the Korean reference translation when the bank has one).
* **F. Fit.** A cell whose prompt exceeds the model window by the tokenizer preflight (prompt tokens + 4,096 retry budget > 65,536) is logged `context_skip` for that model and is not evaluated; it is reported by count. The caps are not lowered afterwards.

**Coverage measure (reported whatever it is; target >= 90%).** A view *holds the expected-answer terms* iff at least one line of the view passes the term test of addendum 31 (`adjudicate.Crit.best_line`: every anchor of the question present **and** >= 50% of the content terms of the bank's expected answer in that one line; whole-word, case-insensitive, whitespace-normalised). Reported: the share of the 188 Stage 1 cells whose view holds them, per variant; the same share for the full rendering (the ceiling) and for the subgraph views of addendum 30 (the confound being removed; read from the committed view files); the share among the cells where a human label differs from the LLM label is computed only in `analyze.py` after labeling. **Because the view builder uses the anchors, the anchor half of the test is true by construction wherever an anchor line exists; the informative half is the answer-term fraction. This is stated so the number is not over-read.** The builder reads the bank's `grounding` (as the paper's own eligibility rule and ablation do) and `question`; it never reads `expected_answer`.

**Stage 2 builder input.** The same rule on the new bank, with the grounding column of the frozen bank.

`personas.py` of this study builds `NEW_BASE` by two asserted string replacements on `BASE`; its sha256 is written to `results.json` and `test_*.py` checks both insertions verbatim against this file. The earlier floor (`R_floor = max(R_raw, 1)` when `S_final = 1`, a post-processing step of the runner of addenda 28/30) is **not applied**: this study uses `R_raw` as the rating, so a rating of 0 is kept even when `S = 1`.

**Per-slot label.** `S_raw` and `R_raw` parsed strictly (`rater_luna.parse_content`); `S_final = 1` only if `S_raw = 1` and the cited span, whitespace-normalised, occurs in the whitespace-normalised **view the model saw** (the span check of addendum 28); an unparsable reply is retried at most twice with the larger budget and then `invalid` (its slot has no label). Every record logs `started_at_utc` and `latency_s` (the timing record), token counts, finish reason; no view text, no address, no key.

**Cell label (primary).** The **majority of the valid `S_final` slots of the 25** (needs >= 15 valid slots; a tie or fewer valid slots leaves the cell **undefined**, excluded and counted). **Cell rating** `R_LLM` = lower median of the valid `R_raw` slots (sorted, element `(n-1)//2`). **Consistency rule R25** (recorded, not a filter): the cell is *consistent* if at least 23 of the 25 `S_final` agree; coverage of R25 and the agreement on the consistent subset are reported next to the primary numbers (never selected after seeing agreement).

**Blindness.** The rater's prompt holds the persona preamble, `NEW_BASE`, the retrieval view, the category, the question and its reference translation. It holds no judge score, no judge rationale, no variant name, no file name, no human label, no draft and no expected answer. Which lines of the view came from a judge span is not marked. The location of the judge's quoted span is the only trace of the judge output in the view, as the author specified; this is a stated limitation (32.12), and the view is never ordered or annotated by it.

## 32.6 Stage 1: calibration gate (pre-fixed)

**Cells.** The 188 primary holdout cells with both human labels (33 question clusters), views by 32.4 with the registered judge's quoted evidence. **Diagnostic, not part of the gate:** the 24 registered ablated controls (the rendering with the anchor lines removed by `ablate_rendered_view`, then the same builder; the registered judge's evidence of that control is located in the ablated rendering): the share labeled `S = 0` by the majority label of each model.

**Labels compared.** `L_m` = the cell label of 32.5 for primary model `m` in {`glm`, `gptoss`}, on its evaluated cells. Human labels `S_AH`, `S_2` (`R2`).

**Gate.** For each primary model `m` and human `h` in {AH, R2}: `kappa(L_m, S_h)` on the evaluated cells of `m` with an interval = question-clustered BCa, 95%, B = 10,000, seed 20260913 (the analyzer's resampling, the `fast_bca` of `review6_reanalysis/common`); `ref = kappa(AH, R2)` **on the same cells** (point estimate) `- 0.10`. The comparison `(m, h)` **passes iff the number of evaluated cells is >= 30 and the lower bound of the interval of `kappa(L_m, S_h)` >= ref.**
**Stage 1 PASS iff at least one primary model passes both humans.** Qwen, the R25 subset, `S_raw` and the persona references do not enter the verdict. A model with fewer than 30 evaluated cells is "not evaluable" and cannot pass.
**Which model's labels carry Stage 2:** every primary model that passes both humans; the *headline labeler* is `glm` if it passes, otherwise `gptoss`. If both pass, both label sets are used and reported side by side; the headline is the headline labeler's.

**Reported with the gate (no threshold attached):** kappa and exact agreement of each `L_m` with each human, kappa(AH, R2) on the same cells, the paired difference `kappa(L_m, h) - kappa(AH, R2)` with an interval; the unsupported-credit rate `pi-hat` = share of cells with registered-judge `J >= 2` and `S = 0` under `L_m`, under AH and under R2 on the same cells, with paired differences and intervals (the paper's values on all 188 cells: AH 7.4%, R2 13.8%); the R25 coverage and the agreement on the R25-consistent subset; the share of label 1; test-retest Krippendorff alpha over the 25 slots; the view coverage of 32.4; the latency distribution. The comparison with the subgraph-view labels of addendum 30 is descriptive.

**If the gate fails:** stop; the report states the gate table and that Stage 2 was not run as an LLM-labeled audit. No threshold, view parameter or rubric is then changed to retry within this study.

## 32.9 Stage 2 gates (the paper's eight gates, same thresholds) and statistics

Each audited judge (`glm`, `gptoss`) is audited separately against the labels of the headline labeler (all four labeler x judge combinations are reported; **the headline pair is the headline labeler with the judge of the other family**, to avoid self-grading). Labels: `S = L_m`, rating `R_LLM`, no floor. Cells: the eligible cells. The analyzer's own functions (`analyze_holdout.analyse` and the helpers it uses) compute the statistics, with seed 20260913, B = 10,000, BCa, question clusters.

| gate | rule in the paper | LLM-labeled analogue here |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | cluster-level one-sided Clopper-Pearson upper bound of silent over-credit (`J >= 2` and `S = 0`) <= 10%, registered level 97.5% one-sided (both levels shown) | same, on the LLM labels |
| G2 | intra-rater QWK, washout, CI lower >= 0.70 | split-half test-retest of the LLM rating: QWK between the median `R_raw` of the seed-1..3 slots (15) and of the seed-4..5 slots (10) of the same model, CI lower >= 0.70 (the LLM has no washout; stated) |
| G3 | rater negative control: >= 22 of 24 ablated cells labeled unsupported | 24 ablated controls drawn from the eligible cells (`Random(20260913)` over sorted cell ids, at most one per cluster, until 24), rendering ablated by `ablate_rendered_view`, same view builder with no judge span; >= 22 of 24 with majority label `S = 0` (an undefined cell counts as a failure) |
| G4 | human-judge QWK, falsification only: fail if CI upper < 0.60; cannot pass | the same with `R_LLM` in place of the human rating |
| G5 | MAE(J, rating) CI upper <= 0.50 | the same with `R_LLM` under the **R = 0 rubric** (no floor) |
| G6 | A->B consistency + non-Qwen judge direction agreement + fragility K >= 6 | same; the "non-Qwen judge" is the other audited judge (both are non-Qwen) |
| G7 | audit trail: 100% of labels logged before unblinding, no post hoc edit, < 3% of cells under 20 s dwell | every label call has `started_at_utc` and `latency_s`; the raw files are append-only and their sha256 is committed with the results; no judge output enters a prompt except the located span (32.5); **the dwell clause is replaced by the logged latency: fewer than 3% of label calls with `latency_s` < 0.5 s** (a reply with a span cannot be produced faster; set without seeing any latency; a cache-hit call is not exempt) |
| G8 | external 36-cell spot check, falsification only | not run (no external check exists for this bank); reported "not computable" |

**Primary report (per judge and per headline pair):** `pi-hat` with question-clustered BCa (seed 20260913, B = 10,000), the **two-way error counts** (`J >= 2` and `S = 0`: over-credit; `J <= 1` and `S = 1`: under-credit; with the totals), MAE (G5) under the R = 0 rubric, coverage on `L1*`, the A->B contrast on `L1*` with the minimum detectable effect `MDE = 2.8 * SD(paired per-question differences) / sqrt(n_pairs)` (the PREREG section 4 form, 80% power, two-sided 5%), the verbatim-span check (share of the judges' `J >= 2` evidence strings that occur verbatim, whitespace-normalised, in the view the judge saw, with `pi-hat` recomputed when a `J >= 2` without a verbatim span is treated as unsupported), and the view term-coverage of 32.4 on the Stage 2 cells.

**Stage 2 label cells and order.** Eligible cells, plus the 24 G3 controls; the primary models that passed Stage 1; 25 slots each. Order: A and B cells first (seeded shuffle `Random(20260913)` of sorted ids), then C-F (same shuffle), controls last. If a wall-clock cap (24 h from the first Stage 2 label call) is reached, the cells completed are the evaluated set, the shortfall is stated, and no further cell is added. Qwen is not run in Stage 2.

## 32.10 Operations, stop rules, records

Resumable JSONL, one record per call; a re-invocation sends only slots without a terminal record. Stop rules as in addendum 28/30: HTTP 402 or 403, an HTTP 429 with Retry-After above 300 s, or 5 consecutive failed calls stop that model's run (no retry loop); context-length replies are `context_skip`. The `qwen` route carries a token budget (150M prompt + completion tokens) and a wall-clock cap (8 h) for Stage 1; no new cell starts after a cap, and the evaluated set is the cells completed (the order is a seeded shuffle that uses no label). A model that is down is recorded as down and skipped. `PROGRESS.md` gets one line with an ETA at each checkpoint (25/50/75/100% of calls of each run, each stage start and end). After Stage 1 completes and `analyze.py` has produced the gate table, a short status (gate result and numbers) is sent to the main session **before** Stage 2 starts.

## 32.12 Limitations (stated before the run)

* The view is built with the bank's grounding terms (as the paper's eligibility and ablation do), so it is favorable to evidence being found; it tests whether the labeler reads that evidence the way the humans did, not whether it could find it unaided. The humans read the full rendering in the paper; the LLM reads at most 100,000 characters of it.
* The located judge span is a trace of the judge output inside the view (the author's specification); no score or rationale is shown, but a rater may be drawn to the span's neighborhood.
* Stage 1 is calibration on 33 clusters; its interval is wide, and a pass is a statement about these cells.
* Stage 2 judges see a subgraph, not the full rendering, and are not the registered judge; the bank is an unreviewed AI draft that was used by code (not read by a person) after it was written.
* The G2, G3, G7 analogues are new constructions; G2 is a split-half retest of sampled ratings, not a washout.
* Persona-ensemble labels at temperature 0.7 are sampled; the label is a majority of 25 correlated draws, not 25 independent raters.
* Shared servers belonging to other projects; weights and load are not controlled.

## 32.13 What this study does not claim

It does not claim that an LLM label is accurate, that the labeler replaces the human raters, that the Stage 2 judges are the registered judge, or that a pass in Stage 1 licenses any other view, bank or domain. It reports what the labeler and the gates produce, as they come out.
