# Addendum 30 -- deviations, quirks and decisions after the freeze (AI-generated; review before use)

Every item is also visible in `PLAN.md` (amendments A1-A4), `SCHEDULE.md` or `PROGRESS.md`; this file lists them in one place. None changed a persona, a seed, a rule (30.6), a metric (30.7) or a threshold (30.8).

1. **Scope and concurrency (A1, A2).** The sets of cells grew from Tier 1 to Tiers 1-3e; the 3-worker limit was replaced by 16 (glm), 24 (gptoss) and 24-30 (qwen) workers chosen by a throughput probe. The GPUs of the two hosts were not cleared and no replica was launched (A2, A3). The prompt order was not changed.
2. **Tier 1 was started at 3 workers per endpoint (before A1) and finished at higher concurrency** after a restart; the runners were stopped by process id and resumed (a call that was in flight at a stop is lost and sent again; terminal records are never re-sent). Labels do not depend on the worker count.
3. **Qwen: wall cap, budget and queue (SCHEDULE, A3, A4).** Started with a 6 h cap and 45M tokens, then 10 h and 450M, then stopped after Tiers 1, 2a, 2b, 3c and 3d (A4); Tier 3a is partial (60 of 213 cells, a random subset by the seeded order), Tiers 3b and 3e were not run for Qwen. The Qwen endpoint was reached through the gateway; it returned HTTP 502 ("timeout elapsed" or a certificate error) on 73 calls, all on long-reasoning cells. After the final resume pass, a slot with two such failures and no reply is an attempted slot without a label (it never agrees); this rule (A4) is implemented in `analyze.py` after the Tier 1 comparison of `glm` and `gptoss` had been run (those two models had no gateway error, so their numbers are not affected; the change touched only `qwen`'s evaluated set and the compressed-raw reader).
4. **Analysis code after the freeze.** `analyze.py` was committed (94e5c9f) before any comparison with human labels. Later commits added the blocks for the extra cells (no human reference; committed before the first run, e4faaef), a gzip reader for the raw files, and the gateway-error rule above. The Tier 1 statistics and the verdict function are unchanged.
5. **Record quirk.** A record with `status: ok` that also has an `error` field means the first attempt (or the second) of that slot was unparsable and a retry succeeded (the `error` key was not cleared); `attempts` says how many requests the slot took. 674 glm slots (537 succeeded on a retry, 137 stayed invalid), 28 gptoss slots and 1,933 qwen slots took more than one request; the analysis uses `status` only.
6. **Pilot.** The 30 pilot calls (`pilot/`) are excluded from every analysis; the same slots were sent again inside Tier 1.
7. **Span check.** The recomputed verbatim check equals the stored flag in every record of every model (0 mismatches).
8. **Views.** The LLM labelers saw the question-relevant subgraph view of each cell (the full rendering for the 6 cells that have none, only for Qwen's window), while the human raters saw the full rendering. This is inherited from addendum 28 (reuse of its views, as instructed) and is not separable from the other differences here.
9. **Second domain (Tier 3d).** Raw call records, views and manifests of the second-domain probe are kept out of the repository (git-ignored `raw_internal/`, `inputs/`, `manifests/mfg*.json`); only aggregates are in `results.json` and the reports.
10. **Raw files** are committed gzip-compressed (`raw/*.jsonl.gz`, deterministic `gzip -n`); `analyze.py` reads either form. They contain no host name, address, port or key (scanned: IP-like strings, `localhost`, `Bearer`, `genai@`, gateway and tunnel ports, `api key`).
