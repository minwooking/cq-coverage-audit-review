## Threats and limits of these reanalyses

- Post hoc, unadjusted, descriptive; no result here changes a registered gate status or the registered A->B decision (unresolved).
- AH labels are LLM-assisted and partly shaped by the AI draft; the second rater saw the judge's span. R1, R2, R5 therefore compare label sources; they do not measure error against a truth.
- L_flip is my construction (raises J to 2 where the label source finds support); L_R uses the rater score, whose floor R>=1 and coupling to S (R determined by S for the second rater) make it a coarse scale.
- R3 'ineligible = 0' is a convention, not a measurement; the ineligible questions are corpus-rare for the variant, so 0 and 3 are the two extreme fills.
- R4 luna is a substitute judge (D28) and its 'limited' condition truncates views; conditions differ in input length, not only in judge. luna full is ceilinged.
- R6: the frozen 48-cell list was chosen for the second-annotator design and holds no A-B pair; nothing can be concluded about A->B on it.
- R7 plug-in worlds resample 33 observed clusters, so they inherit the observed cluster structure; BCa on small discrete data is slightly liberal (6% false-resolution under delta=0 at n=23). G2 and G6 rows are analogues, not evaluations of the registered gates.
- R8 uses one sigma per contrast; the 23-question sigma has a wide interval, so n for equivalence is uncertain by roughly +-40%.
- All 36 reproduction checks pass, but the scripts have not had an independent code audit.
